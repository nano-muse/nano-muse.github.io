What is nanoMuse?nanoMuse 是什么？

nanoMuse is an open-source personal agent in the style of Meta's Muse — one agent with a name and a look of its own, not a toolbox — that runs on the devices you already own and works them for you. nanoMuse 是一个开源的、Muse 风格的个人智能体——一个有名字、有自己形象的智能体，而不是一堆工具——跑在你已经有的设备上，替你操作它们。

  • Every device每一台设备

    Phone, computer and browser are one agent: say it on the phone, it happens on your PC; both ways, with the approvals wherever you are.手机、电脑、浏览器是同一个智能体：在手机上说一句，事情在电脑上办好；双向都行，审批跟着你走。

  • Does things动手做事

    A shell, a browser, MCP servers, skills — and the screen itself as a hand for the apps that never had an API.shell、浏览器、MCP 服务、技能——对那些没有 API 的 App，直接把屏幕当手。

  • Asks first关键处先问

    A stop before deleting, sending or paying, remembered with a scope: once, this chat, or always for this recipient, domain or folder.删除、发送、付款前先停下来问你，并记住范围：只此一次、本次对话，或对这个收件人 / 域名 / 目录一直允许。

  • Listens能听你说

    Say it instead of typing it: a microphone in the composer on the phone, in the browser and on the desktop, and the words land in the box.不想打字就说：手机、浏览器和桌面版的输入框都有麦克风，说的话直接落进输入框。

  • Your coding agents你的 coding agent

    The Cursor, Codex and Claude Code sessions on your computer, seen and steered from your phone or the browser — nothing installed into them, nothing through anyone's server.电脑上 Cursor、Codex、Claude Code 的会话，在手机或浏览器上看、指挥——不往它们里面装东西，也不经过任何人的服务器。

  • Keeps going一直在干

    Goals checked on a schedule, routines that run while the app is closed, a feed written for you every morning.目标按时检查，例程在 App 关着时照样跑，每天早上写给你的动态。

  • Free model use免费的模型额度

    Sign in with a phone number or an e-mail and the agent comes with an allowance of model use, paid for by the project; when it is gone, your own key — Alibaba Cloud Bailian first.用手机号或邮箱登录，账号自带一份模型额度，由项目承担；用完可以换自己的 key，推荐阿里云百炼。

  • Any model任何模型

    Or bring your own: any OpenAI-compatible endpoint, one Model Studio key for chat, image and video, or a provider per model.也可以自己带：任何 OpenAI 兼容接口；一把百炼 key 跑对话、图像、视频，或者每个模型各接一家。

  • Remembers you记得你

    Who it is, what it knows about you, what it remembers and when it wakes: four Markdown files you can read and edit.它是谁、了解你什么、记住了什么、什么时候醒来——四个 Markdown 文件，你都能看、能改。

  • Fully open完全开源

    GPL-3.0-or-later, the whole repository — the app, the desktop, the runtime, even the Cloud relay. Run it all yourself, with no account at all.整个仓库 GPL-3.0-or-later——App、桌面版、运行时，连云中转都在里面。全部可以自己搭，一个账号都不用。

One agent, every device一个智能体，每一台设备

Each device does its own local work and can work the others. They meet through your account on nanoMuse Cloud — or a relay you run yourself. 每台设备都能做本地的事，也能去操作别的设备。它们通过你在 nanoMuse Cloud 上的账号相遇——或者你自己搭的中转。

A window shaped like the phone app, with the same runtime underneath: your files, your terminal, your browser, your screen as a hand — and a Stop that is always one shortcut away. A sentence on the phone runs here; a sentence here can reach the phone.一个和手机版同样形状的窗口，底下是同一个运行时：你的文件、终端、浏览器，屏幕当手——「停止」永远只隔一个快捷键。手机上的一句话在这里执行；这里的一句话也能去手机上办。

Browser浏览器

FAQ

Do I have to install anything?一定要安装吗？

No. Sign in at nanomuse.cn/web with a phone number or an e-mail and you get a nanoMuse of your own running on our server, kept between visits — enough to get a feel for it. Install the phone app (the fullest) or the desktop app when you want it to work your own device — files, terminal, screen. The apps and the web version are the same agent and meet on the same hub.不用。在 nanomuse.cn/web 用手机号或邮箱登录，就有一台属于你的 nanoMuse 跑在我们的服务器上，下次来还在——先感受一下够了。想让它操作你自己的设备——文件、终端、屏幕——再装手机 App（功能最全）或桌面版。App 和网页版是同一个智能体，在同一个 hub 上相遇。

Do I need my own API key?需要自己的 API key 吗？

Not to start. A signed-in account gets ¥10 of model use, free — chat, image and video — through the project's relay; ¥5 more for you and ¥5 for them, for each friend who signs up with your invite code. When it is gone, bring your own key — Alibaba Cloud Bailian is a good start, about two minutes — or connect any OpenAI-compatible endpoint and skip the relay entirely; then nothing of yours passes through anything we run. Your sign-in and your devices keep working either way.一开始不需要。登录后的账号有 ¥10 的免费模型额度——对话、图像、视频——走项目的中转；每邀请一位新用户，你 +¥5、对方也 +¥5。用完后换自己的 key——推荐阿里云百炼，约 2 分钟开通——或者接任何 OpenAI 兼容接口，完全不经过中转；那样你的任何数据都不会经过我们运行的任何东西。无论选哪个，登录和多设备功能都不受影响。

How much does it cost?要花多少钱？

Nothing. nanoMuse is a non-profit community project and will never sell anything under its name. The free model allowance is paid for by the developer; community members who help build it have no cap. Past the allowance you pay only your own model provider, at its prices.不花钱。nanoMuse 是非盈利的社区项目，永远不会以它的名义出售任何东西。免费模型额度由开发者承担；参与共建的社区成员不限量。额度之外，只需按你自己模型服务商的价格付费给它。

Which devices does it run on?能在哪些设备上用？

Android 8.0 or newer (arm64) as an app; Windows 10+, macOS 12+ and Linux x64 as a desktop app or a terminal binary; and any browser through the web version. iOS is planned; until then the web version works on an iPhone too.Android 8.0 以上（arm64）有 App；Windows 10 以上、macOS 12 以上、Linux x64 有桌面 App 和终端版；任何浏览器都能用网页版。iOS 在计划中；在此之前，iPhone 上也可以用网页版。

How do the devices work together?多台设备是怎么协同的？

Every device signs in to the same account and shows up on a hub. A sentence on the phone can run on the computer (its shell, its files, its browser, its screen) and the other way round; results and approvals come back to the device you are holding. A “Stop” on any device stops the hands everywhere. You can run the relay yourself instead of using ours — it is in the repository.每台设备登录同一个账号，出现在同一个 hub 上。手机上的一句话可以在电脑上执行（它的 shell、文件、浏览器、屏幕），反过来也一样；结果和审批回到你手里的那台设备。任何一台上的「停止」会停下所有设备上的操作。中转也可以自己搭，不用我们的——它就在仓库里。

Is it safe to let it use my screen?让它操作屏幕安全吗？

Hands is off by default. When on, the agent looks only at screenshots — never the accessibility tree — and tries a skill, a CLI, an MCP server or the browser first. Before paying, sending or deleting it stops and asks, and it never types passwords or codes: you take over for those. A floating capsule with Stop is on screen the whole time.Hands 默认是关着的。打开后，它只看截图——不读无障碍树——并且先试技能、CLI、MCP 服务或浏览器。付款、发送、删除前会停下来问你，密码和验证码它从不输入，由你接管。整个过程屏幕上都有带「停止」的悬浮胶囊。

What about my data and privacy?我的数据和隐私呢？

On your own devices everything stays in the app's private storage. The relay forwards model calls and keeps a ledger of what they cost; whether it also keeps the text of your chats with the Cloud models, to train the community's own open model, is one switch — Settings → Data controls — and the privacy policy says exactly what that covers, how new accounts start and how to delete it. The web version keeps your data in a container only you can reach, stopped when idle, never read by us. Delete your account at any time.在你自己的设备上，所有东西都留在 App 的私有存储里。中转转发模型调用、记一笔花费；它是否还保存你与 Cloud 模型对话的文字（用来训练社区自己的开源模型），由一个开关决定——「设置 → 数据控制」——隐私政策写清了它具体包括什么、新账号怎么开始、怎么删除。网页版的数据留在一个只有你能访问的容器里，闲置时休眠，我们不会去读。账号随时可以删除。

Is nanoMuse open source? Is it Meta's?nanoMuse 开源吗？是 Meta 的吗？

All of it is open source under GPL-3.0-or-later: the phone app (built on OpenMinis 1.13, every edit marked), the desktop app, the runtime, the web version's gateway and the Cloud relay. It is an independent community project, not affiliated with or endorsed by Meta Platforms, Inc.; Muse is their trademark, and “Muse-style” names the shape we admire.全部开源，GPL-3.0-or-later：手机 App（基于 OpenMinis 1.13，每处修改都有标记）、桌面 App、运行时、网页版的网关和云中转。它是独立的社区项目，与 Meta Platforms, Inc. 无关，也未获其背书；Muse 是他们的商标，「Muse 风格」说的是我们欣赏的那种形态。
