What is nanoMuse?nanoMuse 是什么？

nanoMuse is an open-source personal agent in the style of Meta's Muse — one agent with a name and a look of its own, not a toolbox — that runs on the devices you already own and works them for you. nanoMuse 是一个开源的、Muse 风格的个人智能体——一个有名字、有自己形象的智能体，而不是一堆工具——跑在你已经有的设备上，替你操作它们。

  • Every device每一台设备

    Phone, computer and browser are one agent: say it on the phone, it happens on your PC; both ways, with the approvals wherever you are.手机、电脑、浏览器是同一个智能体：在手机上说一句，事情在电脑上办好；双向都行，审批跟着你走。

  • Does things动手做事

    A shell, a browser, MCP servers, skills — and the screen itself as a hand for the apps that never had an API.shell、浏览器、MCP 服务、技能——对那些没有 API 的 App，直接把屏幕当手。

  • Asks first关键处先问

    A stop before deleting, sending or paying, remembered with a scope: once, this chat, or always for this recipient, domain or folder.删除、发送、付款前先停下来问你，并记住范围：只此一次、本次对话，或对这个收件人 / 域名 / 目录一直允许。

  • Hands over, and waits交给你，然后等

    A login, a code, a payment, a CAPTCHA: the agent hands the page to you and waits for your Done — on the phone's capsule, the desktop's stage, the browser viewer — then goes on from where you left it.登录、验证码、付款、人机验证：智能体把页面交给你，等你点「完成」——手机的胶囊、桌面的舞台、浏览器视图里都行——然后从你留下的地方接着做。

  • In your chat apps在你的聊天软件里

    Your nanoMuse answers in 飞书, 钉钉, 企业微信 and Telegram over the vendors' long connections — no public address, a pairing code for strangers, approvals with a tap.你的 nanoMuse 能在飞书、钉钉、企业微信和 Telegram 里回复，走厂商的长连接——不需要公网地址，陌生人要配对码，审批点一下就行。

  • Your coding agents你的 coding agent

    The Cursor, Codex and Claude Code sessions on your computer, seen and steered from your phone or the browser — nothing installed into them, nothing through anyone's server.电脑上 Cursor、Codex、Claude Code 的会话，在手机或浏览器上看、指挥——不往它们里面装东西，也不经过任何人的服务器。

  • Keeps going一直在干

    Goals checked on a schedule, routines that run while the app is closed, a feed written for you every morning.目标按时检查，例程在 App 关着时照样跑，每天早上写给你的动态。

  • Remembers you记得你

    Who it is, what it knows about you, what it remembers and when it wakes: four Markdown files you can read and edit.它是谁、了解你什么、记住了什么、什么时候醒来——四个 Markdown 文件，你都能看、能改。

  • Fully open完全开源

    GPL-3.0-or-later, the whole repository — the app, the desktop, the runtime, even the Cloud relay. Run it all yourself, with no account at all.整个仓库 GPL-3.0-or-later——App、桌面版、运行时，连云中继都在里面。全部可以自己搭，一个账号都不用。

One agent, every device一个智能体，每一台设备

Each device does its own local work and can work the others. They meet through your account on nanoMuse Cloud — or a relay you run yourself. 每台设备都能做本地的事，也能去操作别的设备。靠你在 nanoMuse Cloud 上的账号，它们就能找到彼此——或者用你自己搭的中继。

Phone手机

The whole agent inside the APK: Alpine Linux, a shell, a browser, MCP, skills, goals on a schedule. With Hands on, it works your apps through the screen — screenshots only — and pauses for you at logins, payments and deletions.整个智能体都在 APK 里：Alpine Linux、shell、浏览器、MCP、技能、按时检查的目标。打开 Hands 后，它通过屏幕操作你的 App——只看截图——登录、付款、删除时停下来等你。

Computer电脑

A desktop app laid out like the Muse desktop, with the same runtime underneath: your files, your terminal, your browser, your screen as a hand — and a Stop that is always one shortcut away. A sentence on the phone runs here; a sentence here can reach the phone.按 Muse 桌面版布局的桌面 App，底下是同一个运行时：你的文件、终端、浏览器，屏幕当手——「停止」永远只隔一个快捷键。手机上的一句话在这里执行；这里的一句话也能去手机上办。

Browser浏览器

Free, open source, non-profit免费、开源、非盈利

The account page shows what is left and how to add more. When it is gone, use your own key — Alibaba Cloud Bailian in mainland China, OpenRouter elsewhere (Bailian only signs up accounts from mainland China). FAQ

Do I need my own API key?需要自己的 API key 吗？

Not to start: a signed-in account has ¥10 of model use, free, plus ¥5 per friend you invite. After that, your own key — Bailian in mainland China, OpenRouter elsewhere — or any OpenAI-compatible endpoint.一开始不需要：登录后的账号有 ¥10 的免费模型额度，每邀请一位朋友再加 ¥5。之后换自己的 key——国内百炼、海外 OpenRouter——或者接任何 OpenAI 兼容接口。

How much does it cost?要花多少钱？

Nothing. nanoMuse is a non-profit community project and will never sell anything under its name. The free model allowance is paid for by the developer; community members who help build it have no cap. Past the allowance you pay only your own model provider, at its prices.不花钱。nanoMuse 是非盈利的社区项目，永远不会以它的名义出售任何东西。免费模型额度由开发者承担；参与共建的社区成员不限量。额度用完后，钱只付给你自己的模型服务商，按它的价格算。

Which devices does it run on?能在哪些设备上用？

Android 8.0 or newer (arm64) as an app; Windows 10+, macOS 12+ and Linux x64 as a desktop app; and any browser through the web version. The iPhone and iPad app is on TestFlight for internal testers; until it is public the web version works on an iPhone too.Android 8.0 以上（arm64）有 App；Windows 10 以上、macOS 12 以上、Linux x64 有桌面 App；任何浏览器都能用网页版。iPhone 和 iPad 的 App 在 TestFlight 内部测试；公开之前，iPhone 上也可以用网页版。

How do the devices work together?多台设备是怎么协同的？

Every device signs in to the same account and shows up on a hub. A sentence on the phone can run on the computer (its shell, its files, its browser, its screen) and the other way round; results and approvals come back to the device you are holding. A “Stop” on any device stops the hands everywhere. You can run the relay yourself instead of using ours — it is in the repository.每台设备登录同一个账号，出现在同一个 hub 上。手机上的一句话可以在电脑上执行（它的 shell、文件、浏览器、屏幕），反过来也一样；结果和审批回到你手里的那台设备。任何一台上的「停止」会停下所有设备上的操作。中继也可以自己搭，不用我们的——它就在仓库里。

Is it safe to let it use my screen?让它操作屏幕安全吗？

Hands is off by default. When on, the agent looks only at screenshots — never the accessibility tree — and tries a skill, a CLI, an MCP server or the browser first. Before paying, sending or deleting it stops and asks, and it never types passwords or codes: you take over for those. A floating capsule with Stop is on screen the whole time.Hands 默认是关着的。打开后，它只看截图——不读无障碍树——并且先试技能、CLI、MCP 服务或浏览器。付款、发送、删除前会停下来问你，密码和验证码它从不输入，由你接管。整个过程屏幕上都有带「停止」的悬浮胶囊。

What about my data and privacy?我的数据和隐私呢？

On your own devices everything stays in the app's private storage. The relay forwards model calls and keeps a ledger of what they cost; whether it also keeps the text of your chats with the Cloud models, to train the community's own open model, is one switch — Settings → Data controls — and the privacy policy says exactly what that covers, how new accounts start and how to delete it. The web version keeps your data in a container only you can reach, stopped when idle, never read by us. Delete your account at any time.在你自己的设备上，所有东西都留在 App 的私有存储里。中继转发模型调用、记一笔花费；它是否还保存你与 Cloud 模型对话的文字（用来训练社区自己的开源模型），由一个开关决定——「设置 → 数据控制」——隐私政策写清了它具体包括什么、新账号怎么开始、怎么删除。网页版的数据留在一个只有你能访问的容器里，闲置时休眠，我们不会去读。账号随时可以删除。

Is nanoMuse open source? Is it Meta's?nanoMuse 开源吗？是 Meta 的吗？

All of it is open source under GPL-3.0-or-later: the phone app (built on OpenMinis 1.13, every edit marked), the desktop app, the runtime, the web version's gateway and the Cloud relay. It is an independent community project, not affiliated with or endorsed by Meta Platforms, Inc.; Muse is their trademark, and “Muse-style” names the shape we admire.全部开源，GPL-3.0-or-later：手机 App（基于 OpenMinis 1.13，每处修改都有标记）、桌面 App、运行时、网页版的网关和云中继。它是独立的社区项目，与 Meta Platforms, Inc. 无关，也未获其背书；Muse 是他们的商标，「Muse 风格」说的是我们欣赏的那种形态。
