nanoMuse

An open-source personal agent for every device you own. 开源的个人智能体，面向你的每一台设备。

nanoMuse is a phone app (Android; iPhone and iPad through TestFlight), a desktop app (Windows, macOS, Linux) and a demo in the browser. It does things rather than answering questions: it can use the screen, and it stops to ask before anything you could not undo. nanoMuse 是一个手机应用（Android；iPhone 和 iPad 走 TestFlight），一个桌面应用（Windows、macOS、Linux），也能在浏览器里体验。它做事，而不只是回答：能看屏幕、动手操作，碰到无法撤销的事会先停下来问你。

The relay, nanoMuse Cloud, signs you in and lets your devices talk to each other. The text of your conversations goes through it so every device shows the same chats; one switch turns that off. The same relay runs on a server of yours. 中继 nanoMuse Cloud 负责登录，并让你的几台设备互相通话。对话文字经它同步，每台设备看到同样的聊天；一个开关就能关掉。同一个中继也可以跑在你自己的服务器上。

Every account starts with a free allowance of model use, paid by the developer. When it is gone, your own key at one of eighteen providers or a ChatGPT plan you already pay for keeps it going. 每个账号都有一份起始的免费模型额度，钱是开发者出的。用完后，换成你在十八家服务商之一的 key，或者用你已经在付费的 ChatGPT 套餐继续。

Free, open source, GPL-3.0-or-later, non-profit. Nothing is sold under this name. 免费、开源、GPL-3.0-or-later、非盈利。不会以这个名字出售任何东西。
