Why nanoMuse为什么是 nanoMuse

Four things define the project. Two are in the app today; two are the next versions. 这个项目由四件事定义：两件已经在 App 里，两件是接下来的版本。

  • today已有

    Muse-styleMuse 风格

    One agent, not a toolbox: a name and a face, a first conversation, a feed written for you, goals worked on in the background, memory you can edit, an approval before anything you could not undo. 一个智能体，而不是一堆工具：有名字、有脸，第一次见面先聊一聊，每天有写给你的动态，目标在后台持续推进，记忆能看也能改，无法撤销的操作前先问你。

  • today已有

    Fully open完全开源

    GPL-3.0-or-later, the whole repository. No closed component, no account, no server you have to trust, no model you have to use. Muse, 豆包 and 千问 are products you are given; nanoMuse is one you own. 整个仓库 GPL-3.0-or-later。没有闭源组件，不用注册账号，不用信任任何服务器，也不绑定任何模型。Muse、豆包、千问是别人给你用的产品；nanoMuse 是你自己拥有的。

  • demo · 0.1.12demo · 0.1.12

    Any app, API or not通用：有没有 API 都能做

    Most of a day in China runs through apps that never had an API. The agent tries a skill, a CLI, an MCP server or the browser first and, when you allow it, the device's own screen — looking at a screenshot and tapping the way you would, never reading the accessibility tree — with the same approvals before paying, sending or deleting. Off by default. 国内日常用的 App，大多从来没有 API。它会先试技能、CLI、MCP 服务或浏览器；你允许之后，再直接操作设备屏幕，只看截图、不读无障碍树，像你一样看、一样点——付款、发送、删除前照样要经你审批。默认关闭。

  • demo · 0.1.13demo · 0.1.13

    Every device多端协同

    One agent, and every device you own is a pair of hands and a front door: say it on the phone, it happens on your PC; say it to your glasses, it happens on both. The phone drives your computer first; a desktop app, iOS, the web on a machine of your own and glasses follow. 一个智能体，你的每台设备都是它的一双手、一个入口：在手机上说一句，事情在电脑上办好；对眼镜说，两边一起办。先做手机操作电脑，之后是桌面 App、iOS、部署在你自己机器上的网页版，还有眼镜。

What it does能做什么

  • Does things动手做事

    A Linux shell, a browser, MCP servers, skills, and — with the accessibility service on — the apps on your phone through their screens, using the accessibility CLI OpenMinis ships. It picks the hand the job needs. Linux shell、浏览器、MCP、技能；开了无障碍服务后，还能用 OpenMinis 自带的无障碍 CLI，通过屏幕操作手机上的 App。用哪只手，它自己挑。

  • Asks first关键处先问

    A stop before deleting, sending or paying, scoped to once, this chat or always — per recipient, domain or folder. Passwords and codes are always yours to type. 删除、发送、付款前先停下来问你：只允许这一次、这次对话，或者对这个收件人 / 域名 / 目录一直允许。密码和验证码永远由你自己输入。

  • Keeps going一直在干

    Goals checked on a schedule in their own conversation, routines that run while the app is closed, the screen kept awake when it drives the phone, and a "continue?" instead of an early wrap-up. 目标在各自的会话里按时检查；例程在 App 关着时照样跑；操作手机时屏幕不会熄灭；到了步数上限会问你「继续？」，而不是草草收尾。

  • Writes you a feed写给你的动态

    Every morning, a few short posts from what it knows about you and what you asked it to follow. Steer it with one sentence. 每天早上几条短帖，来自它对你的了解和你让它留意的事。说一句话就能调整方向。

  • Remembers you记得你

    Who it is, what it knows about you, what it remembers and when it wakes are four Markdown files you can read and edit. Bring what another assistant knew. 它是谁、了解你什么、记住了什么、什么时候醒来——四个 Markdown 文件，你都能看、能改。别的助手对你的记忆，也可以直接导进来。

  • Has a face有一张脸

    Describe one, your image model draws four, you pick. It breathes at rest, bobs while it works, tilts when it needs you, pops when a task lands. 描述一句，你的图像模型画四张，你挑一张。休息时呼吸，工作时点头，需要你时歪头，任务完成时跳一下。

How it works怎么工作

On the phone在手机上

Built on OpenMinis: a Linux root file system under proot, a shell, a browser, MCP, skills, scheduled tasks and an accessibility executor — inside the APK, no server, no cloud VM. Your files and memory never leave the device. 基于 OpenMinis：proot 下的 Linux 根文件系统、shell、浏览器、MCP、技能、定时任务和无障碍执行器——都在 APK 里，没有服务器，没有云端虚拟机。你的文件和记忆不离开手机。

Your model你的模型

Any OpenAI-compatible endpoint, or the OAuth sign-ins the app ships with. Messages go only to the model you configured; the avatar is drawn by an image model on the same account. 任何 OpenAI 兼容接口，或者 App 自带的 OAuth 登录。消息只发给你配置的模型；形象也由你自己配的图像模型来画。

Open开放

GPL-3.0-or-later, one small version at a time, each an installable APK with its notes. Not affiliated with Meta; Muse is a trademark of Meta Platforms, Inc. GPL-3.0-or-later；按小版本逐个发布，每个版本都是可安装的 APK，附更新说明。与 Meta 无关；Muse 是 Meta Platforms, Inc. 的商标。
